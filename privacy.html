Carter privacy policy

Carter is a private assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own machines.

With the owner's permission, the app reads the owner's own Google Drive files and Gmail messages, read-only, when the owner asks the assistant a question. It does not write, send, share, move or delete anything in the owner's Google account.

Data read through the app stays on the owner's own computers. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or for training any model. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
